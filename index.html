Retention · Customer lifetime value

Which subscribers should we try to keep, and with what offer?

A targeted offer, not a general discount. An annual contract with one free month for month-to-month customers adds about USD 48k; a 15% discount for all of them would lose about USD 239k. The power analysis shows that a pilot with the current base cannot confirm the break-even, and how higher take-up would change that.

  • Logistic regression
  • Discounted LTV
  • Expected value

Experimentation · Freemium monetization

Should we move the freemium gate?

No. Moving the first gate from level 30 to level 40 loses 3–4% of the players still active after a week. I also found an imbalance in how players were assigned and showed the result holds after adjusting for it.

  • Power analysis
  • SRM check
  • Two-proportion z-test
  • Bootstrap
  • Sensitivity analysis

About

For seven years I have analysed how customers and markets respond to prices, offers and costs. I built the sourcing, pricing and margin analysis a multi-brand apparel retailer in Uruguay and Argentina used to choose its suppliers. At Georgia-Pacific I worked on pricing, forecasting and margin. At a subscription TV operator I tracked churn by cohort and designed a retention strategy the board adopted.

Each case study here starts with a business decision and ends with a recommendation, with the code and the checks behind it in the open.
